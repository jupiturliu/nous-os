Walkthrough · for students, parents, teachers

How a student learns with AI without losing the ability to think.

A seven-step loop. The student leads. AI assists. The system remembers what helped and what didn't. Each step shows what AI is doing, what the student is doing, and the line AI must not cross.

Student (human-led)
AI (assists)
System memory
Boundary AI must not cross

What stays human

01

The question

The student picks what they're curious about. AI does not decide what's worth learning.

02

The judgment

When AI says "this is true" the student checks. Important claims need sources, not just confidence.

03

The choice

Health, money, school, relationships — decisions with real consequences stay with the human.

04

The values

AI can advise. It cannot decide what life is worth pursuing.
